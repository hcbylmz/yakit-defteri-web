How to calculate MPG

The full-tank method gives the most accurate number:

  1. Fill the tank to the top and note the odometer.
  2. Drive as usual.
  3. Fill the tank to the top again. Note the gallons and the new odometer reading.
  4. Subtract the two odometer readings. That is how far you drove on those gallons.

Formula: MPG = miles ÷ gallons

Example: you drove 320 miles and the tank took 10.4 gallons. 320 ÷ 10.4 = 30.8 MPG. At $3.29 a gallon that is 10.4 × 3.29 ÷ 320 = $0.107 per mile.

What about a partial fill-up?

Don't calculate a partial fill-up on its own. Add up all the gallons until the next full tank and divide the miles over that stretch by the total. A single partial fill-up gives a number that is far too high or too low.

Why is my dashboard MPG different?

The trip computer estimates fuel use, and on many cars it reads a little better than reality. The number from the pump is the real one.

MPG to L/100 km

L/100 km = 235.2 ÷ MPG (US gallons). 30 MPG is about 7.8 L/100 km. For UK gallons use 282.5 instead of 235.2.
